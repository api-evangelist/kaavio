A few days ago I wrote that B2B buyers expect deep product understanding and tailored customer insights to make informed purchasing decisions. 🧠 It’s about more than listing technical specs—it’s about delivering all the information customers need, exactly when they need it, in a way that makes the most sense (like 📊 product comparison tables). Delivering this level of product experience comes with substantial challenges, especially when product data is inconsistent or incomplete.
