Every distributor we talk to has the same story. Someone on the team sits behind three monitors copying data from one spreadsheet to another, trying to wrangle product information for tens of thousands of SKUs from hundreds of suppliers. If the company has grown through acquisitions, it's worse: duplicate part numbers, conflicting attribute schemas, no unified catalog.
